# HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI, DAX & Excel

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing employee data to understand workforce trends and identify key factors contributing to employee attrition. Using Power BI, I developed an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard to track employee metrics, analyze attrition patterns, and provide insights that support data-driven HR decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze employee demographics and workforce distribution.
- Track key HR metrics and employee attrition rates.
- Identify departments and employee groups with higher attrition.
- Present actionable insights to support employee retention strategies.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **DAX** – Calculated measures and KPI development.
- **Microsoft Excel** – Dataset handling and data preparation.

## 📊 Dataset Overview
- **Total Records:** 1,470 employees
- **Total Attributes:** 24 columns
- **Domain:** Human Resources
- **Key Attributes:** Age, Gender, Department, Job Role, Education, Marital Status, Business Travel, Monthly Income and Attrition.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
1. Total Employees
2. Active Employees
3. Total Attrition
4. Attrition Rate
5. Average Age
6. Average Monthly Income

### Visualizations
- Employee attrition by gender.
- Attrition analysis by department.
- Employee distribution and attrition by age group.
- Attrition by job role.
- Attrition by education field.
- Employee-related breakdown using a matrix.

### Interactive Filters
- Gender
- Marital Status
- Education
- Business Travel

## 🔍 Key Insights
- **Overall Attrition:** 16.1% of employees (237 employees) left the organization.
- **Department Analysis:** Sales recorded the highest departmental attrition rate at 20.6%.
- **Age Analysis:** Employees under 25 had the highest attrition rate at 39.2%.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Focus on employee retention strategies within the Sales department.
- Develop targeted engagement programs for employees under 25.
- Monitor attrition trends across departments and job roles to support better HR decisions.
